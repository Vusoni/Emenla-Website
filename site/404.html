Emenla

An iPhone app for people with endometriosis. Your words, their words, one clear page, kept on your phone.

Made in Poland.

Intended use
Emenla is a record-keeping app. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Nothing in the app or on this site diagnoses, treats or predicts anything.

Images
The people, paper and light on this site are generated images, not photographs, except where a source is credited. Nobody shown is a patient.
