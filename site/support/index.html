Get in touch

The fastest way is inside the app: Settings, then Give feedback. It goes to the same person, and it comes back with a status you can see, read, planned, shipped, or will not do, so you know what happened to it rather than wondering.

Or by email: hello@emenla.com.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. Emenla asks you to sign in with Apple, with Google, or with an email address and password before you start. The account is how you get back in, and it is what will make the Community section possible later.

It does not sync, back up or transmit your symptom record. Everything you log about your body is written to your phone and stays there whether you are signed in, signed out or offline.

Where is my data kept?

Your symptoms, period dates, body map and notes are kept on your device, in the app's own private storage.

Two things live on a server in Frankfurt, Germany: your email address, and, only if you use the meal reading in Nourish, four nutrition figures per scanned meal. Nothing else. The privacy policy sets out exactly what happens on every path.

What happens when Nourish reads a photo of a meal?

That one photograph is sent to OpenAI to be read, and four figures come back: energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat, with how confident the reading was. Those figures are stored against your account.

The photograph is not stored by us. It is not written to any database, any file store, or any log. OpenAI reads it as our processor, under a data processing agreement, and does not use it to train its models. If you never scan a meal, no photograph ever leaves your phone.

How do I get my record out?

Two ways, both in the app. Record, then Document, then Create a dossier builds the doctor summary as a PDF and hands it to the share sheet, so you can print it, email it or send it. Settings, then Export gives you everything you have recorded as a file.

How do I delete everything?

Settings, then Delete all my data. It asks twice, then deletes your Emenla account, your email address and every meal-reading figure, and permanently removes the app's database from your device.

It deletes the account first: if it cannot reach the server it stops and deletes nothing, so you are never left with an emptied phone and an account still on file.

Will I lose my entries if I change phone or delete the app?

Yes. Your symptom record lives only on the device it was made on, and the account does not back it up. Export your data before deleting the app or moving to a new phone.

Does Emenla tell me whether I have endometriosis?

No, and it never will. Emenla documents, organises and educates. It does not diagnose, treat or predict anything. Every figure it shows is a count of something you recorded, shown with what it is out of.

Why does it ask for the microphone?

So you can say how you feel instead of typing it, which is easier mid-flare. Speech is transcribed by your device, on your device. No audio is uploaded and no recording is kept. You never have to use it; typing does exactly the same thing.

Why does it ask for the camera or photos?

So you can attach a photo of a meal or a test result to something you log. An attached photo is copied into the app's private storage and stays there. The single exception is the meal reading above, which you have to tap to start.

Can I turn off the food section?

Not as a switch. When you set the app up, Emenla asks how you feel about tracking food. If you answer that it is a difficult area or that you would rather skip it, Nourish shows you the reading library and nothing else: no logging and no plans, anywhere in the app, until you change it yourself.

Will it remind me to log?

Only if you ask it to. One reminder in the evening, scheduled by your own device, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time. There are no streaks anywhere in Emenla and there never will be.

Something looks wrong, or a button does nothing

Please write, with the screen name and what you were doing. Settings, then Give feedback is the quickest. Reopening the app fixes most display problems and never loses anything.

What is sent when I use Give feedback?

What you wrote, your email address, and four things about your phone: the version of Emenla, the version of iOS, your iPhone model and your language. The app lists those four on the screen before you send.

Nothing from your record goes with it. No entry, no symptom, no date, no photograph, no summary. Deleting your account deletes your feedback with it.

Is something not working, or is it only me?

Settings, then Service status. Almost all of Emenla runs on your phone and cannot be affected by anything on our side: writing, symptoms, the body map, your cycle, Overview and the doctor summary all work with no connection at all. Only two things need one, reading a meal from a photograph, and signing in or changing your account. That page says which, and checks while you look at it.
