2. What Emenla is, and what it is not

Emenla is a documentation tool. It helps you record symptoms with very little effort, see counts and patterns in your own data, and prepare a summary you can take to an appointment.

Emenla is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It is a documentation tool: everything it shows you is a count of what you recorded, not an interpretation of it, and nothing here can tell you whether you have endometriosis or any other condition. Always talk to a qualified clinician about your symptoms, and before changing anything about your care.

That is not a disclaimer bolted on to cover us. It describes how the app is actually built, and you can check it as you use it:

  • Nothing in Emenla produces a diagnosis, a risk score, a forecast about your health, or a judgement about whether a day was good or bad.
  • Where Emenla shows you a figure, it is a count of what you logged: never a percentage of your body, never a grade, never a direction of travel.
  • The clinical terms Emenla shows you are your own words matched to recognised clinical vocabulary, printed with your sentence beside them so anyone can check the match. They are a translation, not an interpretation.
  • The summary you can produce says this on the page itself: it is a patient's own record, contains no diagnosis, and no part of it has been interpreted by the app.

Emenla cannot tell you whether you have endometriosis, and it will never try. If you are unwell, or something changes, or you are worried, speak to a clinician. In an emergency, contact your local emergency service. Emenla is a diary, and nobody monitors it.

It does not sync or back up your record. If you sign in with Apple and use Hide My Email, we receive a relay address and the app works exactly the same way.

A few things we ask:

  • Use one account, for yourself. Do not share it, and do not create an account for someone else without their knowledge.

One thing to know before you sign in on a phone that is not yours. Emenla keeps your record on the device, so a device can only belong to one account at a time. If you sign in on a phone whose record belongs to a different account, Emenla will ask you to confirm, and confirming permanently erases the record already on that phone. There is no backup and no way to recover it. Read that prompt carefully: it is the only place in Emenla where signing in destroys something.

Emenla also offers an optional App lock, which asks iOS to check Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode when you return to the app. iOS does the checking and only tells Emenla whether it passed. Because your record lives on your device and nowhere else, anyone with access to your unlocked phone can read it. We strongly recommend a device passcode.

9. Automated features, and their limits

Emenla contains three features that use an AI model: the meal reading in Nutrition, the assistant, and Analyze on the appointment page. All three are optional, and if you use none of them, nothing about your body ever leaves your phone.

When you ask Emenla to read a meal from a photograph:

  • The photograph is sent to OpenAI or Anthropic to be read, through a server in the EU. Only the photograph is sent: no symptoms, no notes, no dates, and nothing identifying you.
  • We do not store the photograph. It is never written to any database, file store or log of ours, and it stays on your phone, where you took it. The Privacy Policy says what each of them may keep, and for how long.
  • Four figures come back (energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat) with a confidence level, and those are stored against your account.
  • The figures are estimates from one photograph, not measurements. The app says so on the screen where they appear. They can be wrong, especially on a crowded or badly lit plate, and you can correct any of them.
  • Nothing about this reading is medical. It estimates nutrition. It says nothing about your symptoms, your condition or your care.

When you talk to the assistant:

  • Nothing is sent until you agree. The first time, a notice in the conversation says where your messages go. If you choose "Not now", your messages are read on your phone instead and nothing is sent.
  • Your message is sent to OpenAI or Anthropic to be read and answered, through a server in the EU. Your symptom record is never sent with it. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what goes with each message.
  • We do not store your messages. We keep a timestamp for each one so we can keep a daily limit, and nothing about what you wrote.
  • Its answers are written by a model and can be wrong. It gives no diagnosis and no medical advice. Check anything that matters with a clinician or a source you trust.
  • Nothing it suggests is saved until you save it. It can offer an entry or an appointment, and you see it and choose before anything goes into your record.

When you tap Analyze on your appointment page:

  • Nothing is sent until you agree. Analyze opens a page with a Use AI switch that says where your questions go, and it is off until you turn it on. While it is off, the suggestions are written on your phone and nothing is sent.
  • The questions you wrote are sent to OpenAI or Anthropic, through a server in the EU, with the names of your symptoms and a few short sentences the app writes from your record, such as how many days you logged a symptom on. Your entries themselves are never sent. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what goes.
  • We do not store your questions or what comes back. We keep a timestamp so we can keep a daily limit, and nothing about what you asked.
  • What comes back is written by a model and can be wrong. The app checks every line on your phone first, and drops one that carries a number not in the facts it was given, or a condition you did not write yourself. It gives no diagnosis and no medical advice.
  • Nothing lands on your page until you tap it, and your own wording can be put back with one tap.

Nothing else in Emenla is AI, and in particular the clinical translation is not. The clinical terms Emenla finds in your words come from a fixed, hand-written vocabulary and a set of rules that look for specific phrases. The same words always give the same result, no model is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere. A term is only shown after the rules match your own sentence, and before a term reaches a summary you confirm it yourself. No part of Emenla ever concludes anything about your health.

Voice notes are transcribed by your iPhone, on the device. No audio and no transcript is sent to us or to anyone else.

Two things need a connection: signing in, and the meal reading. When either is unavailable, everything else keeps working. Emenla has a Service status page in Settings that tells you which of the two is reachable.

We do not promise that Emenla will be available at all times or free of faults. There will be bugs. We will fix what we can, as fast as we can. If something is broken, please tell us through the feedback page, which goes straight to a person.
