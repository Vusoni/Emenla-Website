# The bar: alveoslabs.com home page, plus the augen hero screenshot

Read from 20 full-width screens of alveoslabs.com at 1440x900 (17,336px tall) on 2026-08-27, and the
augen screenshot the founder supplied. Every line is something a critic can check by looking.

## Mechanisms (what makes it good)

1. **Every section has a picture, and the pictures are people.** 13 of the 15 sections carry a
   photographic image or video of a person; the two that don't are the stat cards and the FAQ.
   The images are warm, natural light, real-looking people in real places, and the product is
   always on them or in their hand. Nothing is an icon-with-a-headline.

2. **One canvas, one dark moment, one dark close.** The page is one cream (#fcf9f7) from the trust
   strip to the FAQ. Exactly two sections break it: the hero (a warm tan spotlight that fades down
   into the cream) and the footer (near-black). Cards are pure white on the cream, corners 25px,
   shadows barely there.

3. **The signature is a drawn line that draws itself.** The "7am to 10:30pm" section is a hand-drawn
   wave across the whole screen with numbered nodes; the part you have scrolled past is black, the
   part ahead is light grey, and each node's card is fully opaque only when the line reaches it.
   Future cards are ghosted at about 30 percent. Nothing else on the page uses that line.

4. **Type: three sizes do all the work.** Headlines 36 to 58px at weight 500, body 18 to 20px in a
   mid grey, and 12px tracked uppercase for labels and pills. Headlines are two lines at most and
   centred; body under a headline is centred too, at about 68 characters. Section headings never
   exceed 9 words.

5. **No two neighbouring sections share a layout.** Centered statement, then a wave, then a 2+3
   photo grid, then photo-left text-right, then text-left photo-right, then a full-bleed dark photo
   card with a frosted sub-card, then a centered heading over a huge dark card, then four stat
   cards, then three quote cards, then a video card, then a founders grid, then FAQ left-heading
   right-accordion, then a bento, then the dark close.

6. **Text over photos always sits on a bottom-up dark gradient**, white headline at 28 to 36px
   weight 600, one short body line under it, centred at the bottom of the card. The top 40 percent
   of every photo card is clean image with no text.

7. **Motion is quiet and one-directional.** Elements fade up 16 to 24px as they enter, once. The wave
   draws in the scroll direction. The hero product floats slowly. Nothing bounces, nothing loops
   faster than a breath, nothing moves for under 400ms, and nothing moves while you read a paragraph.

8. **One button style, repeated.** Near-black pill-ish button (12px radius), white text, used at the
   top right, under the hero, after the stats, after the quotes, in the footer. A white outlined
   twin appears only beside it. No third style.

## The augen hero (the founder's look for the woman)

9. **A person dissolving into the canvas.** A single woman in profile, eyes closed, calm, on a
   near-white ground, the image fading to nothing at the bottom edge so there is no rectangle,
   no card, no border; she is part of the page. The type is small and quiet (a wordmark and one
   short line at the bottom left). More than 60 percent of the frame is empty light.

## Where our page stands today (so the gap is honest)

- Ours has 2 generated images and 1 video, none of a person. Alveos has 13 sections with people.
- Ours has a timeline rail (a straight line) where Alveos has the drawn wave with live cards.
- Ours has one photo-grid-style card. Alveos has five photo cards in one grid plus four more below.
- Ours matches on canvas, corners, button style, type, and section-to-section layout variety.
- Ours has a proper hero video and captions where Alveos has a still product. That is our advantage.

## Emenla's own style, so this is not a copy

- People, but ours: a small cast of two or three women, generated, shown the augen way: high-key
  light, near-white or cream ground, edges dissolving into the page, eyes closed or turned away,
  never a stock pain pose, never a clinic. Calm and specific, like someone at 7am on a bad day.
- One accent: violet, only for her own sentences and the voice headlines. In the imagery it appears
  once per scene at most, as a thread of light, a hairline, a ribbon of fabric.
- The signature line is not a wave: it is a **cycle**, a soft loop drawn across the screen with
  day-nodes, because her patterns are counted across cycles. It draws as you scroll, and each node
  carries her words and the clinical term beside them.
- Loops breathe: any looping clip moves at the pace of a breath or slower (a 4 second cycle or more),
  and returns to its first frame so the join is invisible.
